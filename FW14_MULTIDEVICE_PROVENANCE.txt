HARDWARE CLAIM BOUNDARY
-----------------------
No physical-console execution result is claimed as evidence for this publication. Source inspection defines a successful browser result as dispatch reaching pthread_create; visible GoldHEN readiness would require a separate hardware observation.

Moving the closure to GitHub Pages changes origin, routing, caching, and server-side controls. Publication and desktop-browser smoke tests do not prove execution on a physical PS4. Any console attempt must be reported as a new observation, not inferred from deployment success.
